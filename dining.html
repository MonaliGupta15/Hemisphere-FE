Culinary Collection

Flavors of Sarawak. Rooted in heritage & coastal freshness.

From legendary Halal Sarawak Laksa and handcrafted Szechuan delicacies to fresh seafood grills by the South China Sea.

Our Restaurants & Bars

Discover seven distinctive culinary destinations across four iconic addresses.

Culinary Collection • 7 Destinations
Grand Margherita Hotel
Kuching Waterfront Halal Certified

Orchid Garden Coffee House

The definitive home of authentic Halal Sarawak Laksa in Kuching. Enjoy all-day interactive buffet spreads, Sarawakian street specialties, and tranquil riverfront views.

Signature Dish: Original Sarawak Laksa
Service: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Buffet
Hours: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM
Grand Margherita Hotel
Level 1, GMH Halal Szechuan

Meisan Szechuan Restaurant

Kuching’s premier Halal-certified Chinese restaurant specializing in fiery Szechuan wok specialties, crispy aromatic duck, and weekend dim sum banquets.

Signature Dish: Crispy Szechuan Duck & Dim Sum
Atmosphere: Imperial Chinese Decor & VIP Rooms
Hours: 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM, 6:30 PM – 10:30 PM
Damai Beach Resort
Beachfront Veranda Open-Air Sea View

Cafe Satang

Open-air coastal dining overlooking the South China Sea and Satang Island. Savor fresh charcoal seafood grills, Sarawak black pepper beef, and tropical fruit refreshments.

Signature Dish: Santubong Seafood Platter
Atmosphere: Ocean Breeze & Rainforest Canopy
Hours: 7:00 AM – 10:30 PM
Damai Lagoon Resort
Lagoon Edge Sunset Pavilion

Lagoon Terrace

An elegant al fresco pavilion hovering above the resort lagoon. Serving contemporary Southeast Asian fusion dishes infused with wild Borneo botanicals and artisanal marinades.

Signature Dish: Torch Ginger Glazed Salmon
Atmosphere: Tranquil Water Reflection & Sunset
Hours: 6:30 AM – 10:00 PM
Riverside Majestic Puteri
Puteri Wing Cantonese Fine Dining

River Palace

Exquisite Cantonese fine dining overlooking the Sarawak River. Celebrated for mastercrafted dim sum, double-boiled cordyceps broths, and fresh steamed Empurau fish.

Signature Dish: Steamed River Silver Pomfret
Atmosphere: Opulent Imperial Banqueting
Hours: 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM, 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM
Riverside Majestic Astana
Rooftop Floor Panoramic Cocktails

The Sky Lounge

Perched high above the river, featuring craft mixology infused with Tuak reductions and wild Borneo herbs. The pinnacle of Kuching nightlife with curated vinyl sessions.

Signature Drink: Santubong Sunset Negroni
Atmosphere: Intimate Skyline Glow & Jazz
Hours: 5:00 PM – 1:00 AM

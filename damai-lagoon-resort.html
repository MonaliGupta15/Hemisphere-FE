The Sanctuary

Calm Lagoon Waters & Sunset Horizons

Damai Lagoon Resort was conceived as a restful coastal sanctuary where modern luxury meets the unhurried tempo of the Borneo coastline. Overlooking a tranquil sheltered lagoon cove, the resort offers private suites with daybeds, infinity edge pools, and personalized wellness rituals.

From candlelit dinners over the water at Lagoon Terrace to traditional herbal body scrubs at the Mandara Spa, every moment is calibrated for restoration and romance.

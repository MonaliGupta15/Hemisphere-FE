Sarawak Experiences • 4 Signature Pathways
01 • UNWIND
Coastal Serenity & Wellness

Beachside Respites & Botanical Wellness

Awaken to the soothing rhythms of gentle waves on the Santubong Peninsula. Rejuvenate your spirit with indigenous herbal compresses, open-air yoga pavilions, and barefoot strolls on the golden sands of Damai Beach.

Signature Mandara massage with virgin Borneo coconut oil
Private lagoon daybeds and sunset infinity pool relaxation
Complimentary sunrise mindfulness sessions on weekends
Explore Damai Lagoon Resort
02 • SAVOR
Gastronomic Heritage

Sarawakian Spices & Artisanal Culinary Journeys

Sarawak is UNESCO’s creative city of gastronomy. Delve into the rich complexity of wild forest fern (Midin), crushed white and black pepper from upland hills, freshly pulled Halal Sarawak Laksa, and aromatic Cantonese dim sum.

Masterchef Laksa tasting experience at Orchid Garden
Handcrafted Halal Szechuan Peking Duck banquets at Meisan
Rooftop craft Tuak cocktail mixology at Sky Lounge
Explore All Dining Venues
03 • EXPLORE
Untamed Nature & Marine Escapes

Ancient Rainforests & Coastal Marine Safaris

Trek through primary dipterocarp rainforest under the shadow of mythical Mount Santubong, spot rare Irrawaddy dolphins along the Salak mangrove estuary, or take an adventurous day excursion to the rugged sea-stacks of Bako National Park.

Guided canopy treks departing directly from Damai Beach Resort
Sunset dolphin cruise across the Santubong Peninsula
Walk to the adjacent Sarawak Cultural Village living museum
Explore Damai Beach Resort
04 • CONNECT
Living Heritage & Riverfront Vitality

Kuching Waterfront Trails & Artisanal Guilds

Step out of Grand Margherita or Riverside Majestic and immerse straight onto the historic Kuching Waterfront. Wander through centuries-old Chinese shophouses on Main Bazaar, observe traditional songket weavers, and cross the Darul Hana Bridge at golden hour.

Direct walking access to Main Bazaar antique shops and craft guild
Sampan river crossing to Fort Margherita and Orchid Garden
Adjoining connectivity to Sarawak Plaza shopping arcade
Explore Grand Margherita Hotel

Sarawak's First

A Waterfront Landmark Built on Heritage

Grand Margherita Hotel holds an indelible place in Kuching’s modern history. Situated on the scenic bend of the Sarawak River, it commands unobstructed views of the historical Astana Palace, Fort Margherita, and the vibrant waterfront esplanade.

Celebrated as the epicenter of Halal gastronomy in Sarawak, GMH welcomes global delegates and local families alike with the legendary Sarawak Laksa at Orchid Garden and celebrated Peking Duck at Meisan Szechuan Restaurant.

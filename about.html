The Brand & Mission

One Hemisphere. Rooted in Sarawak, inspired by excellence.

As the hospitality arm of the Sarawak Economic Development Corporation (SEDC), Hemisphere unites the region’s premier destinations under a shared promise of timeless luxury.

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Sarawakian Hospitality

Hemisphere Corporation Sdn Bhd was established to steward, innovate, and elevate the distinguished hospitality properties under the SEDC Sarawak Group of Companies. Our portfolio connects the wild natural grandeur of the Santubong Peninsula with the energetic riverfront vitality of historic Kuching.

While industry icons like Anantara and Dusit demonstrated how unified multi-property brand ecosystems deliver world-class guest journeys, Hemisphere preserves the individual soul of each landmark: the traditional beach chalets of Damai Beach, the modern tranquility of Damai Lagoon, the pioneer heritage of Grand Margherita, and the dual-wing executive presence of Riverside Majestic.

Our Pillars

Responsibility to Nature & Community

Hospitality with a profound reverence for Sarawak’s environment.

Canopy & Marine Protection

Active stewardship of the Mount Santubong coastal fringe. Single-use plastic reduction across our beach resorts and collaboration on Irrawaddy dolphin preservation.

Hyper-Local Sourcing

Over 80% of our fresh seafood, indigenous mountain rice (Bario), and botanicals are procured directly from Sarawakian smallholders and coastal artisanal fishermen.

Bornean Artisan Support

Showcasing authentic indigenous crafts, rattan basketry, and Pua Kumbu textiles in our hotel interiors, while providing sustainable careers for local communities.

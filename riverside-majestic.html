The Puteri Wing & The Astana Wing

Choose between classic riverside luxury or high-tech contemporary boutique comfort.

Puteri Wing • Classic Luxury

The Puteri Wing

A Kuching institution offering timeless Malaysian hospitality, solid timber architectural touches, and spacious suites overlooking the tranquil river. Home to River Palace Chinese Restaurant and the Puteri Grand Ballroom.

Puteri Executive Club floor with private lounge check-in
Award-winning River Palace Cantonese gastronomy
Puteri Grand Ballroom seating up to 1,000 delegates

The Astana Wing

Sleek, design-conscious, and technology-empowered. Designed for corporate achievers and design connoisseurs with digital room controls, marble rainfall showers, The Sky Lounge rooftop mixology, and modern hybrid auditoriums.

Rooftop Sky Lounge featuring vinyl jazz and craft cocktails
Modern interactive Astana Conference Center with video walls
Astana Club Lounge with complimentary sunset wines
